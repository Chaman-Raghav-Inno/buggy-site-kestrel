Company policies

How Kestrel Works handles hours, leave, expenses and pay. All amounts are in euros.

Timesheets

  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday. Enter hours per project for every day you worked, as decimals (7.5) or hours and minutes (7:30), in quarter-hour steps. A day can't have more than 24 hours.
  • The week's total includes every day, weekends too. A standard week is 40 hours; hours over 40 in a week are overtime. Long days don't count as overtime on their own.
  • Submitting a week sends it to your manager and locks it. If it's rejected, the manager's reason is shown on the week and you can fix it and submit again.

Leave

  • Everyone gets 25 days a year. Up to 5 unused days carry over from last year; anything beyond 5 is lost.
  • Leave is counted in working days: weekends and the public holidays below don't use any allowance. A half day uses 0.5.
  • You can't book more than your available balance, book days you've already requested, or pick an end date before the start.
  • Pending and approved requests come off your balance straight away. Cancelling upcoming leave gives the days back.
  • The team calendar shows approved and pending leave, every day of it, never rejected requests.

Expenses

  • Claim within 90 days of the expense. Amounts in other currencies are converted to euros: {{rates}}.
  • Anything over €25 (after conversion) needs a receipt.
  • Meals are reimbursed up to €50 a claim.
  • Mileage pays €0.45 a km for your first 10,000 km in the calendar year, then €0.25 a km.
  • "Awaiting approval" on the expenses page is the total of your pending claims.

Pay

  • You're paid a twelfth of your salary on the 25th of each month. Your hourly rate is your salary ÷ 2,080.
  • Overtime is paid at 1.5 × your hourly rate.
  • Approved expenses are repaid with your salary. They aren't income, so they aren't taxed.
  • Income tax is 20% of taxable pay (salary and overtime). Pension is 5% of your monthly salary.
  • "Gross pay this year" on a payslip includes that month. Payslips are private: only you can see yours.

Approvals

  • Managers approve their team's timesheets, leave and expenses. Nobody approves their own requests: a manager's own claims go to their manager.
  • Rejecting anything needs a reason, which the employee sees.
